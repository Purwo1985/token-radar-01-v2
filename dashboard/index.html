    A note to the reader

    The board's job is to find protocols whose token does not exist yet, rank them by an implied value it states, and show how to farm each one. This page does that from one snapshot and one fitted line, and it shows its working: the fit, the walk-forward error, the per-category residuals and the factor waterfall behind every single implied value. Where the evidence is thin — a weak R², a wide out-of-sample error — it says so in the open, because a radar that hides its error is not a radar.
